Advanced Electronics Technology Courses

Embedded Systems

Learn microcontrollers, sensors, communication protocols, and embedded programming. Build practical real-world electronic systems and smart devices.

02

IoT & Smart Systems

Explore connected devices, sensors, cloud platforms, and wireless communication. Develop smart solutions for homes, industries, and automation.

03

VLSI Design

Learn digital design, Verilog, RTL concepts, and semiconductor fundamentals. Gain practical knowledge for designing and developing integrated circuits.

PCB Design

Learn schematic creation, component placement, routing, and PCB layout techniques. Develop professional skills for designing reliable electronic circuit boards.
